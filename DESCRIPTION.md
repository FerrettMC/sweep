## Full (about 2,400 characters — Play long description, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub)

Sweep is a shopping app for Android that searches several stores at once and
tells you whether a discount is actually a discount.

ONE SEARCH, FIVE STORES

Type what you're after and Sweep searches Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, eBay and
Etsy at the same time, then shows what each one is charging side by side.
Results appear as each store answers rather than waiting for the slowest. If a
store doesn't sell that kind of thing, Sweep says so rather than quietly
returning less.

IT CHECKS WHETHER THE SALE IS REAL

This is the part Sweep exists for. A shop sets the original price and the shop
sets the discount, so a red "40% off" badge is the shop's word for both. Sweep
keeps its own record of what things have cost, so it can tell you when that
badge is sitting on the price something has been all month.

IT WATCHES PRICES FOR YOU

Track a product and Sweep checks it for you and tells you when the price drops.
If notifications are switched off, or you missed one, the record is still in the
app waiting.

DEAL RADAR

Tracking answers "tell me when this gets cheaper". A radar answers "tell me when
anything matching this turns up under $X", which is the question you actually
have before you've picked a listing. Describe what you want, set a price, and it
keeps looking for weeks.

PRODUCT LOOKUP

Paste a link from any supported store and Sweep reads the listing: the price,
the ratings, what buyers actually said split into what they liked and what they
didn't, the price history, and similar products that might cost less.

LISTS, CART AND BUDGET

Build a shareable gift list or wishlist with live prices from different shops and
see what the whole thing costs. Keep a cart of what you've decided to buy,
gathered from wherever you found it. Log what you actually spent.

NO AI IN THE PRICE PATH

Every number in Sweep is read from the store's own listing, never predicted. When
a shop doesn't publish something, the app leaves that section empty and names the
shop rather than filling the gap with something plausible. Most shopping
assistants launched this year do the opposite.

FREE, PROPERLY

The free tier is 10 searches and 12 product lookups a day, 3 tracked products,
price history, lists and the budget tracker. No card, no trial that turns into a
charge, nothing that expires. Nothing interrupts you while you use it, and the
only ad in the app is one you can choose to watch for an extra search past the
daily limit. Pro and Ultimate raise the limits.

Sweep is built by one 16-year-old developer. The support email goes to the
person who wrote the code.
