# Where to submit Sweep

Free, no pay-to-play. Work down the list; the top half is worth more than the
bottom half by a wide margin.

Rule of thumb from people who do this a lot: **three or four on launch week,
then one or two a quarter.** Firing at forty in a day is a pattern, and
patterns get filtered.

---

## Directories — do these first

**AlternativeTo** — alternativeto.net
The single highest-value one for Sweep, and it is not close. The site ranks for
"[product] alternatives" searches, which is exactly how people find a tool like
this. List Sweep as an alternative to:

    CamelCamelCamel · Keepa · Honey · Karma · Slickdeals · ShopSavvy · Rakuten

Somebody googling "CamelCamelCamel alternative" has already decided they want
this category and is one click from installing. That traffic compounds forever
and costs nothing.

**SaaSHub** — saashub.com
Same mechanic, ranks for alternatives queries. Set and forget.

**Product Hunt** — see sweep-ads/producthunt/LAUNCH.md. Account now, launch in
two weeks.

**Indie Hackers** — indiehackers.com
Post the build story rather than the product. The 16-year-old solo build and
the two stores that died in production is the post; Sweep is the footnote.

**Launching Next** — launchingnext.com. Free, permanent link.

**Slant** — slant.co. Community recommendations, answers "what is the best
price tracker" style questions.

## Press — contact forms, no deliverability problem

These take a form rather than an email, so nothing lands in spam and nothing
needs the domain's mail set up properly.

| Where | Route |
| ----- | ----- |
| XDA Developers | xda-developers.com/contact/ → "News Tip" |
| Droid Life | droid-life.com/contact/ → **"Apps"** category |
| Android Headlines | androidheadlines.com/news-tips |
| Phandroid | phandroid.com — has a form specifically for app review requests |
| Clark.com | clark.com/contact/ — consumer money, best shopping-side fit |
| The Penny Hoarder | thepennyhoarder.com/contact/ |
| The Krazy Coupon Lady | thekrazycouponlady.com/contact |

Short version to paste, since most forms cap the length:

> I'm Jude, 16, and I built an Android app called Sweep. It searches Amazon,
> Walmart, Best Buy, eBay and Etsy in one go, and because it keeps its own price
> history it can tell you when a "40% off" badge is sitting on the price
> something has always been. Free, 10 searches a day, no card.
> https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sweepshopping.app

## Skip

- **G2, Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice.** Real sites, wrong audience. They
  are B2B software buyer directories; nobody shops for a consumer price tracker
  there.
- Anything calling itself "200+ app review sites". Pay-to-play SEO farms, and
  several will invoice you.
- Anything asking for money to "feature" the app.
