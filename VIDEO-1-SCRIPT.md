# Video 1 — the fake sale

The strongest one, and the only claim a competitor can't copy. Shoot this first.

**Length:** 15–18 seconds. **Format:** vertical 1080×1920. **Voiceover:** optional.

---

## Before you record

1. Open **`https://api.sweepshopping.com/video`** in a browser
2. Full-screen it (F11), press **H** to hide the key hints
3. The stage scales to fill whatever window it's in — no cropping needed
4. **Space** advances one beat, **R** resets. Nothing moves on its own, so you
   can retake without reloading

Record with OBS, your phone, or any screen recorder set to a vertical region.

---

## The beats

| Time | Press | What's on screen | Caption to overlay |
| ---- | ----- | ---------------- | ------------------ |
| 0:00 | Space | Amazon product, **40% OFF**, $239.99 struck from $399.99 | **"This says 40% off."** |
| 0:04 | Space | Price history draws — a flat line for a month | **"It's been that price for a month."** |
| 0:09 | Space | The verdict: *this isn't really a sale* | **"So it's not a sale."** |
| 0:14 | Space | Sweep wordmark, "Know when a sale is real" | **"Sweep — free on Google Play"** |

Hold each beat about 4 seconds. Long enough to read, short enough not to drift.

---

## If you talk over it

Keep it flat and unimpressed — the screen is doing the work.

> "Shops do this constantly. A big discount badge, sitting on the price the
> thing has been all month. There's usually no way to check — so I built one.
> Sweep keeps its own price history, so it can tell you when a sale isn't one."

Roughly 14 seconds read at a normal pace. Don't rush it to fit; cut a sentence
instead.

---

## Captions

Burn them in — most people watch muted. Big, high contrast, top third of the
frame so a thumb doesn't cover them.

---

## What NOT to say

- **Don't call it a real deal you caught.** It's a demo of what the app does,
  built with plausible data because Sweep is too new to have a month of real
  history yet. "Here's what Sweep does" is honest; "here's one I found" isn't.
- **Don't say "the cheapest price"** — say "the stores we check".
- **Don't name a store Sweep doesn't actually search.**
- **Don't say "we".** One person made this, and saying so is the appeal.

---

## Reshoot this later

Once real price history exists — a month or two of tracking — retake it with a
genuine product and real recorded data. The whole pitch is that the data is
real, and a demo built from real data is a stronger video anyway. Keep this
version until then; replace it when you can.
