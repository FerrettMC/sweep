# Video 1 — the fake sale

The strongest one, and the only claim a competitor can't copy.

**Length:** ~15 seconds. **Format:** vertical, filmed on your phone.
**You talk, the screen is b-roll.**

---

## Recording it

1. Open **`https://api.sweepshopping.com/video`** on your phone
2. Screen record it — it's one screen, nothing to press, nothing to wait for
3. Talk over it, or film yourself and cut to the screen recording

That's the whole setup.

---

## What to say

Open on the screen, not on you.

> "Say you find a deal like this — forty percent off, looks great.
>
> Sweep keeps its own price history, so it can show you the thing's actually
> been that price for a month.
>
> So it's not a sale. It's just the price.
>
> That's the whole app. It's free."

About 15 seconds at a normal pace. Don't rush to fit — cut a line instead.

---

## The hook matters most

The first two seconds decide whether anyone watches. Strong openers:

- "Say you find a deal like this."
- "This says forty percent off. Watch."
- "Shops do this constantly and nobody checks."

Weak openers, for the avoidance of doubt: "Hi guys", "So I built an app",
anything starting with your name.

---

## Captions

Burn them in — most people watch muted. Top third so a thumb doesn't cover
them.

---

## The one thing to get right

**Don't say "I just found this deal."** The screen is a demo built with
plausible data, because Sweep is too new to have a month of real history yet.
"Say you find a deal like this" is the same hook and is true.

The whole pitch is that the data is real. That's worth protecting in the
fifteen seconds where it's easiest to spend.

Also:
- "the stores we check", never "everywhere" or "the cheapest price"
- don't name a store Sweep doesn't actually search
- don't say "we" — one person made this, and saying so is the appeal

---

## Reshoot in a month

Once real history exists, retake it with a genuine product and real recorded
data. Stronger video, and then every word is literally true — including "I
found this."
