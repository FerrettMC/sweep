Open it on your phone, start recording, read this over it.

Timings are the b-roll's own. You don't have to hit them exactly — every line
is written short so there's room to breathe, and the captions carry the meaning
if you drift.

## Delivery

**Flat, not salesy.** The first line is a complaint, not a pitch — say it the
way you'd say it to a friend. The whole thing only works if it sounds like
someone who got annoyed and fixed it, which is what actually happened.

**Land on "actually" and "means it."** Those two words carry the whole claim.
Everything else can be thrown away.

**Don't rush 0:25.** That's the payoff line. There's 4 seconds for 9 words.

**The opening has room now.** Six seconds for that first line, which is about
1.5 seconds more than you need — so you can land it, pause, and let the tabs
finish stacking before "So Sweep…". The pause is the joke.

## If you want a stronger hook

Swap the first line for one of these — same length, more of an opinion:

- "Half the sales you see aren't sales."
- "I'm sixteen and I got tired of being lied to by shopping apps."

Both are riskier and both travel further. The second one only works if you're
on camera for it.

## If you want real Amazon and eBay footage

The opening is browser tabs rather than recreations of their storefronts, on
purpose — naming shops you actually search is fine, but rebuilding someone
else's shop inside a page carrying your brand is a different thing, and not
worth it for a promo.

If you want the real thing, film it yourself: open Amazon and eBay on your
phone, screen-record ten seconds of the same product at two prices, and cut it
over the first six seconds. Your own footage of you shopping is unambiguous,
and it will look better than anything I can fake. The tab scene is built to be
replaced this way — everything after 0:06 is Sweep and stands on its own.

## Notes

- The captions are burned in, so don't add your own — they'll collide.
- Record in a quiet room; the b-roll has no audio, so there's nothing to hide
  behind.
- One take is fine. It loops, so if you fumble a line just keep going and use
  the second pass.
