# Videos

Six ideas, strongest first. Each is one idea, shot vertically, 15–30 seconds.

**Two rules that matter more than the scripts:**

1. **Hook in two seconds.** Not "hi guys, I built an app" — start on the thing
   that makes someone stop scrolling.
2. **Show, don't narrate.** A screen recording of a fake sale being caught
   explains Sweep faster than any sentence about it.

**Film now, post at launch.** Sweep is in closed testing, so a video that lands
today sends people to a link they can't install. Having four ready on launch day
is worth more than posting one now.

---

## 1. The fake sale — make this one first

The only claim no competitor can copy, and it's inherently visual.

> **[0:00]** Screen recording: a product page, big red **40% OFF** badge.
> **On screen:** "This says 40% off."
>
> **[0:03]** Open the same product in Sweep. Price history chart fills in flat.
> **On screen:** "It's been that price for a month."
>
> **[0:08]** Zoom on Sweep's verdict line.
> **On screen:** "Sweep keeps its own price history. Stores can't fake that."
>
> **[0:12]** Cut to black.
> **On screen:** "Sweep. Free on Android."

No voiceover needed. If you do talk: *"Shops do this constantly and there's
usually no way to check. So I built one."*

## 2. Why the free tier has limits

Unusual enough to travel on its own — almost no app explains its own costs.

> **[0:00]** **On screen:** "Every app hides why it limits you. Here's mine."
> **[0:03]** Screen recording scrolling the "Why are features limited?" page.
> **[0:10]** Stop on the running-costs section.
> **Voiceover:** "Amazon charges me per search. eBay and Etsy don't. The server
> costs the same either way. That's the whole reason there's a limit."
> **[0:20]** **On screen:** "10 free searches a day. No card. No trial."

Honest, specific, slightly odd — which is why it works.

## 3. "I'm 16 and I built a shopping app"

The story, told straight. Works where a feature list gets scrolled past.

> **[0:00]** Face to camera. *"I'm sixteen. I built a shopping app because I
> got annoyed at fake discounts."*
> **[0:05]** Screen recording of a search fanning out across stores.
> *"It checks a few shops at once and keeps its own price history."*
> **[0:15]** *"No investors, no company. Costs me about £X a month to run."*
> **[0:22]** *"It's free. Link's below."*

Don't oversell. The restraint is the appeal.

## 4. What buyers actually said

Shows product lookup, and it demos well because the data is genuinely dense.

> **[0:00]** **On screen:** "Reading 40,000 reviews, in three seconds."
> **[0:02]** Paste a link into Sweep. Product page loads.
> **[0:05]** Scroll to the review section — the green/amber keyword chips.
> **On screen:** "Sound quality ✅ · Battery 🟡 · Fit 🟡"
> **[0:12]** **On screen:** "It doesn't summarise them with AI. That's what
> buyers actually said."

## 5. No AI

Timely, and a real differentiator while every other shopping app ships a chatbot.

> **[0:00]** **On screen:** "Every shopping app is adding AI. Mine doesn't have any."
> **[0:04]** Screen recording of a search returning real listings.
> **Voiceover:** "AI shopping assistants make up products that don't exist and
> prices nobody charged. Sweep reads the actual listing."
> **[0:14]** Scroll to a section that's empty because a store publishes nothing.
> *"And when a shop doesn't tell us something, it says nothing instead of
> guessing."*

That last beat is the strongest half — an empty section is proof.

## 6. The notification

Short, satisfying, and shows the thing people actually keep an app for.

> **[0:00]** Phone on a desk. Lock screen. Nothing happening.
> **[0:02]** Notification slides in: "£40 off the thing you're watching."
> **[0:05]** Tap it — Sweep opens on the product, price history showing the drop.
> **[0:10]** **On screen:** "Track it once. It watches for you."

## Practical notes

- **Vertical, 1080×1920.** Same file works on TikTok, Shorts and Reels.
- **Screen record on the phone**, not an emulator — emulators look fake.
- **Use a real product**, ideally something people recognise.
- **Captions always.** Most people watch muted.
- **One video, one idea.** Two ideas in thirty seconds is zero ideas.

## Claims to keep straight on camera

Same guardrails as `SELLINGPOINTS.md`, because these are easier to break when
talking:

- "the stores we check", never "everywhere" or "the cheapest price"
- "up to every hour", never "every hour"
- Don't name a store Sweep doesn't currently search
- Don't say "we" — there's one of you, and saying so is the point

## Where to post

- **TikTok / Shorts / Reels** — ideas 1, 4 and 6 are the visual ones
- **r/frugal, r/deals, r/androidapps** — lead with ideas 2 or 3, never a
  feature list. These communities dislike being marketed at and respond well to
  someone showing their homework
- **Play listing** — a 30-second version of idea 1 as the feature graphic video
