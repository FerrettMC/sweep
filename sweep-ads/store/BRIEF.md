# Play Store screenshots

Five raw captures at 1080x2424, straight off a Pixel running the live app.
Real data, real prices, nothing mocked up.

The notification-icon strip has been blanked out. Clock, wifi, signal and
battery are real but pinned by Android's demo mode, so every shot matches.

## Order matters

Play shows only the first two or three in search results, before anyone taps
through. Keep this order.

| # | File | Text on the image |
| - | ---- | ----------------- |
| 1 | store-1-search.png | Search five stores at once |
| 2 | store-2-lookup.png | See if the sale is actually a sale |
| 3 | store-3-tracking.png | Know the moment a price drops |
| 4 | store-4-radar.png | Name your price. Sweep watches for it. |
| 5 | store-5-lists.png | Lists that work across every store |

## For the designer

- 9:16, minimum 320px and maximum 3840px on the longest side. These are fine as-is.
- Feel free to place them in a device frame and add a background.
- Brand colours: background `#0D0D0D`, surface `#1A1A1A`, accent `#D85A30`.
- Please don't crop out the price or the store name on any shot. Those are the
  proof, and they are the reason the screenshots work.

## What is actually in each one

**1 search** — one query fanned out to Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, eBay and
Etsy. Note "Skipped Etsy — they don't sell this kind of thing." That line is
deliberate and should stay visible if the crop allows.

**2 lookup** — a real Amazon listing at $155 down from $263.86, with the ratings,
"3K+ bought in past month" and "#3 best seller". The "Is this sale real?"
heading is at the bottom of the frame.

**3 tracking** — a tracked product with "Same as when you started", which is the
app being honest that nothing has changed yet.

**4 radar** — a standing search for airpods pro 2 under $180, with "Best seen:
$39.99".

**5 lists** — three items across two different stores with a running total of
$373.90. The cross-store total is the point of this one.

## Not included yet, on purpose

A price history chart. The database was cleared at launch on 2 September, so
nothing has more than a few days of history and every chart is currently a flat
line with two points on it. Worth adding a sixth screenshot in a few weeks once
there is a real curve to show. Faking one is not an option, given the app exists
to catch exactly that kind of thing.
